00 / Prologue

Ansh,
In Draft.

I’m interested in how intelligent systems represent information, how they behave, and what happens when we try to understand or change either.

My recent work has taken me from representation learning and model evaluation to behavioural steering and the security of stateful AI agents. I’m currently exploring questions where these threads meet—especially those relevant to making increasingly capable systems safer.

Share-borne AI Virus

Memory-hopping attacks across LLM agents

Research on how adversarial content can travel through persistent memory and shared artefacts between otherwise independent assistants—examining injection, propagation, reinfection, and multi-hop persistence in controlled agent workflows.

Sycophancy Is Steerable

Learning behavioural directions with RL

An ongoing investigation of per-layer steering vectors trained with reinforcement learning and an LLM judge, while keeping the base model frozen. Further evaluation and sanity checks are part of the work.
